Welcome to the Real Jungle Book: Tadoba Andhari Tiger Reserve

Spanning over 1,727 sq. km in Chandrapur district, Maharashtra, Tadoba is renowned globally for having one of the highest densities of wild Bengal tigers in India (~115+ tigers). Derived from local deity "Taru" and the meandering Andhari River, the park features dense dry deciduous teak forests, serene lakes, rocky hills, and rich bamboo thickets.

Best Time to Visit & Seasonal Guide

Plan your safari according to wildlife sighting odds, weather conditions, and seasonal landscapes.

Peak Sightings: March – June

Oct – Feb (Winter)

10°C - 28°C

Pleasant weather with lush green foliage after monsoons. Migratory birds arrive around Tadoba Lake. Great for general safari comfort and birdwatching.

Tiger Sighting Index: Moderate (7/10)

Mar – Jun (Summer)

32°C - 45°C

Hot weather dries up interior forest waterholes. Tigers and wildlife frequently congregate around remaining lakes (Tadoba, Telia, Jamunbodi) for hours.

Tiger Sighting Index: Highest (10/10)

Jul – Sep (Monsoon)

24°C - 32°C

Core gates remain closed during heavy rains for wildlife breeding. Select buffer zones remain open and offer breathtaking green landscape drives.

Core Availability: Closed (Buffer Open)

Tadoba "Big 5" Wildlife Showcase

Key flagship species found across Tadoba's diverse ecosystems, hot zones, and sighting tips.

High Sighting Frequency
Apex Predator

Royal Bengal Tiger

Home to over 115 tigers. Tadoba offers unparalleled opportunities to witness tigers cooling off in lakes or patrolling territories.

Primary Habitat: Tadoba Lake, Telia & Pandharpauni
Best Season: March – June (Waterholes)
Rare Sight: Melanistic

Melanistic Leopard (Black Panther)

Featured here is Tadoba's exceptionally rare melanistic leopard (black panther). While standard leopards inhabit rocky outcrops, this rare melanistic variant is occasionally sighted quenching its thirst near reserve waterbodies.

Primary Habitat: Agarzari, Shivanzari & Kolsa Rocks
Rarity Status: Rare Melanistic Gene Mutation
Unique Sighting

Sloth Bear

Tadoba is one of the premier parks to observe sloth bears foraging for termites or feeding on Mahua flowers in early summer.

Primary Habitat: Kolsa Range & Zari Sector
Best Season: March – May (Mahua Bloom)
Endangered Pack

Dhole (Indian Wild Dog)

Formidable pack hunters known for coordinated hunts. Frequently seen along jungle tracks near Moharli and Navegaon grassland edge.

Primary Habitat: Navegaon & Moharli Grasslands
Best Season: November – April
Mega Herbivore

Gaur (Indian Bison)

The largest species of wild cattle in the world. Massive herds roam the bamboo thickets around Tadoba Lake and Andhari River basin.

Primary Habitat: Bamboo thickets & River basins
Best Season: October – May
